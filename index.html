Bench Sales Analytics — 6 UX Prototypes

Same underlying dataset (165 candidates, 6 recruiters, 5 platforms, 8 roles, 10 clients, 24 months of submissions/interviews/offers/placements) rendered through six different analytics UX philosophies. Open each one, apply the same filters, and compare which structure surfaces answers fastest.

Today (simulated): Sep 12, 2026 History: Sep 2024 – Sep 2026 Data: shared/data.js (seeded, deterministic)
★ UNIFIED — RECOMMENDED PRODUCT DIRECTION
Best of All
Combines the strongest pattern from each prototype into one product: Executive KPI hierarchy (01) + power filtering & drill-down (02) + CRM pipeline with rate confirmation (03) + deep candidate intelligence & individual profiles (04) + a needs-attention workflow (05) + the business/revenue layer (06). Every chart is clickable and cross-filters the whole dashboard.
01 — EXECUTIVE INTELLIGENCE
Executive Intelligence
Senior-management view: 8-metric KPI band, operating trend, candidate funnel, health mix, platform & role leaderboards, recruiter scatter, narrative insights.
"How is the entire bench-sales operation performing?"
02 — POWER ANALYTICS
Power Analytics
BI-style exploration: dense grid, right-side multi-select filter drawer, click-to-drill-down on any chart, per-chart menus (view data / compare / export).
"Let me investigate the data myself."
03 — SALES PIPELINE / CRM
Sales Pipeline
CRM-style horizontal pipeline with stage-to-stage conversion, bench-aging distribution, and a live candidate pipeline table sorted by days-in-stage.
"Where is every candidate in the sales process?"
04 — CANDIDATE INTELLIGENCE
Candidate Intelligence
Deepest candidate focus: leaderboard, performance quadrant, activity heatmap, and a full individual-candidate profile with 8 dedicated charts.
"Which candidates are working, and which need help?"
05 — OPERATIONS CONTROL CENTER
Operations Control Center
Action-first: attention summary tiles, activity velocity, a "needs attention" worklist ranked by signal, and recruiter workload balance.
"What needs my attention right now?"
06 — STRATEGIC BUSINESS
Strategic Business Analytics
Revenue-first view: pipeline value, forecast funnel, revenue by technology/recruiter/candidate, and revenue-vs-submissions efficiency scatter.
"What is driving revenue and placements?"
07 — VISUAL ANALYTICS
Visual Analytics
Chart-form-matches-data-shape: sparkline KPI tiles, a stacked-area volume trend, donut/pie composition charts, a polar-area role breakdown, and a radar comparing recruiters across five dimensions at once.
"What does this data actually look like, chart by chart?"
08 — SIMPLIFIED ANALYTICS
Simplified Analytics
Built for ease over completeness: just 4 tabs, a 2-control top bar (everything else lives in one Filters drawer), an attention strip before any chart, and a candidate slide-over panel so drilling into a person never loses your place in the page.
"Just tell me what I need to know, quickly."
How to compare fairly: every prototype exposes Period, Compare, Granularity, Recruiter, Candidate, Platform, Role and Client controls reading from the same shared/data.js dataset. Set the same filters in two prototypes to see how differently each surfaces the same underlying answer.
